Waiting for the new Moon

★★★
Grade 1
Name:Class:Date:
Learning goals
  • Read about the Moon in our culture.

On the last evening of Ramadan, Imran's family went up to the roof after sunset. Everyone looked at the western sky.

"There it is!" shouted Imran. A very thin new Moon was shining low in the sky. "Eid Mubarak!" said his grandmother. Tomorrow would be Eid.

1
Where did the family look for the Moon?
in the western sky, after sunset
2
Why was seeing the new Moon important?
It showed that Ramadan was over and Eid would start tomorrow.
