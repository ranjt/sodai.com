I am a young scientist

★☆☆
Grade 1
Name:Class:Date:
Learning goals
  • Say how scientists find things out.
  • Know how to stay safe.

Scientists are curious. They ask questions and find the answers by looking closely, measuring and testing. You can do this too!

How scientists work

1. Ask a question
2. Guess what will happen
3. Test it
4. Record what happens
5. Tell others

Tools we use

hand lens: look closely
ruler: measure
timer: how long
thermometer: how hot

Is it fair?

To find out which ball bounces highest, we drop every ball from the same height. If we threw one ball harder, the test would not be fair.

Stay safe

Listen to your teacher. Never taste or smell things unless you are told it is safe. Wash your hands after a test.

Little scientist at the pond

★★★
Grade 1
Name:Class:Date:
Learning goals
  • Read a story.
  • Think like a scientist.

Nadia saw tiny black wriggly things in the village pond. "What are they?" she asked. She looked closely with a hand lens and drew them in her notebook.

Every week she went back and drew them again. The wriggly things grew legs. Then they lost their tails. They were baby frogs, called tadpoles!

1
What question did Nadia ask?
2
How did Nadia record what she saw?
  • She drew pictures.
  • She took them home.
3
What were the wriggly things?
