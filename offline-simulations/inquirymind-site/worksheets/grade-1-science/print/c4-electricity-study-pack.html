Electricity and circuits

Learning goals
  • Name the parts of a circuit.

Lights, fans, phones and televisions use electricity. Some things get electricity from the wall socket. Some use batteries (cells).

lamp
fan
TV
phone
torch
Stay safe

Electricity from the wall is dangerous. Never touch sockets or wires, and keep water away from them.

A simple circuit

A circuit is a loop. It has a cell, wires and a lamp. Electricity flows round the loop from the cell, through the lamp and back. The loop must be complete, with no gaps.

A switch makes a gap in the circuit (off) or closes the gap (on).

Where does electricity come from?

sunlight (solar panels)
wind
moving water
burning gas or coal
Key words
circuit
a complete loop for electricity
cell
a battery that stores electricity
switch
turns a circuit on and off

Light from the sun

★★★
Grade 1
Name:Class:Date:
Learning goals

Shuvo lives on a char, an island in a big river. There are no electricity wires there.

His family has a solar panel on the roof. In the day it turns sunlight into electricity and stores it in a battery. At night the battery lights two lamps, so Shuvo can do his homework.
