Changing shape

We can change the shape of some materials by squashing, bending, twisting and stretching.

ActionTry it with
squasha sponge, clay, play dough
benda pipe cleaner, a straw
twista towel, a rope
stretcha rubber band, a balloon

Some materials go back to their shape (a sponge). Some stay in the new shape (clay).

Real-world case study · C1 · Materials

The potter of Rajshahi

★★★
Grade 1
Name:Class:Date:
Learning goals
  • Read about a natural material.

Mr Paul is a potter. He digs clay from the river bank. He squashes and shapes the soft wet clay on his spinning wheel to make pots.

Then he dries the pots in the sun and heats them in a hot fire called a kiln. Now the clay is hard and will never go soft again.

1
Is clay a natural material or made by people?
  • natural
  • made by people
2
How does Mr Paul change the shape of the clay?
