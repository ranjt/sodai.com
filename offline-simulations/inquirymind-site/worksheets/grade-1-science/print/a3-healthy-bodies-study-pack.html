Healthy bodies

★☆☆
Grade 1
Name:Class:Date:
Learning goals
  • Say what a balanced diet is.
  • Name ways to keep healthy.

To stay healthy we need good food, exercise, clean hands and rest.

A balanced diet

A balanced diet has some of each kind of food every day, and plenty of water.

FoodWhat it doesExamples
energy foods (carbohydrates)give us energyrice, roti, potato
body-building foods (proteins)help us growfish, egg, meat, dal, milk
protecting foods (vitamins, minerals, fibre)keep us healthyfruit, green vegetables
fats and oils (a little)store energyoil, butter, nuts

For the grown-up

Rub a little oil and glitter on children's hands (pretend germs). Try washing: water only for 5 seconds, water only for 20 seconds, soap for 20 seconds. Count glitter spots left.
