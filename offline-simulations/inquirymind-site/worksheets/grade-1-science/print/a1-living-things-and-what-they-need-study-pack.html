What makes something living?

★☆☆
Grade 1
Name:Class:Date:
Learning goals
  • Name what all living things do.
  • Say what living things need.

A cat, a mango tree and you are all living things. A rock and a chair are not. What is the difference?

All living things…

move
breathe
feel and notice
grow
have young
get rid of waste
need food

Plants do these things too! A plant grows towards the light, takes in water through its roots and makes seeds. Inside animals, blood carries food and air around the body.

Living things need…

food
water
air
light and warmth
shelter
Key words
living
alive: it grows, moves, feeds and has young
non-living
never alive, like a stone
needs
things it must have to stay alive

Is the stone alive?

★★★

Learning goals

Arif found a smooth stone by the river. "It is alive," he said, "because it rolled down the hill!"

His sister Lina laughed. "Does it eat? Does it grow? Can it have babies?" Arif thought hard. "No," he said. "The stone is not alive. The water pushed it."
