Heating, cooling and dissolving

★☆☆
Grade 1
Name:Class:Date:
Learning goals
  • Describe how heating and cooling change things.
  • Find things that dissolve.

Some materials change when we heat them or cool them.

When we heat it…When we cool it…
ice melts into waterwater freezes into ice
chocolate and butter meltmelted chocolate goes hard again
an egg cooks and goes harda cooked egg stays cooked
water boils and turns to steamsteam turns back into water drops
ice
chocolate
egg
hot water

Some changes can go back (ice ↔ water). Some changes cannot go back (a cooked egg).

Dissolving

When we stir sugar or salt into water, it seems to disappear. It has dissolved. Sand and rice do not dissolve.

salt dissolves
sugar dissolves
sand does not
rice does not
Stay safe

Only grown-ups use the stove and hot water.

Key words
melt
change from solid to liquid when heated
freeze
change from liquid to solid when cooled
dissolve
mix into water and seem to disappear

Making pitha in winter

★★★
Grade 1
Name:Class:Date:
Learning goals
  • Read about heating food.

In winter, Grandma makes pitha. She mixes rice flour with water to make a soft dough. She melts date palm gur (jaggery) in a pan.

She steams the pitha over hot water until it is cooked. The soft dough changes into a firm cake. Yum! The changes from heating cannot go back.
