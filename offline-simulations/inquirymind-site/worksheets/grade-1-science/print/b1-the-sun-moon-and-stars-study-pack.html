The Sun, the Moon and the stars

★☆☆
Grade 1
Name:Class:Date:
Learning goals
  • Say how the Sun seems to move.
  • Explain day and night.

Every morning the Sun rises in the east. It climbs high in the sky at midday, and in the evening it sets in the west.

Shadows are long in the morning and evening, and short at midday when the Sun is high.

Why do we have day and night?

The Sun does not really move. The Earth spins! The side facing the Sun has day. The side facing away has night. You can show this with a torch and a ball.

The Moon and stars

At night we can see the Moon and the stars. The Moon also rises and sets. Its shape seems to change during the month.

Bangladesh connection

The Sun and Moon matter to people. Muslims break their fast at sunset during Ramadan, and Eid begins when the new Moon is seen. Pohela Boishakh welcomes the new year of the Bangla calendar.

Stay safe

Never look straight at the Sun. It can hurt your eyes.
