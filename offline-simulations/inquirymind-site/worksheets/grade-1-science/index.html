Study packs and worksheets

Print-ready, black-and-white study packs that cover the whole Grade 1 science syllabus. Each pack teaches the topic with an illustrated article, then builds from facts to thinking, a real investigation and a transdisciplinary case study set in Bangladesh. Every pack has its own answer key.

How to use the packs

Print

A4, black and white, double-sided. Every worksheet starts on a new page, so you can print a single sheet or the whole pack.

Learn

Start with the Learn it article and its key words. Learners can read it in class or at home before the activities.

Practise

Stars show the challenge level: ★☆☆ core, ★★☆ deeper, ★★★ stretch. Marks are shown for each question.

Check

Use the answer key to mark or for self-checking. The last page of each pack is a self-assessment: “Not yet, Nearly, Got it!”

Syllabus coverage map

Every Grade 1 learning outcome in the school science continuum (IB PYP Phase 2) and the pack that teaches it. Working scientifically skills are also practised in every pack's investigation.

Working scientifically · 6 outcomes in 1 pack
PackGrade 1 learning outcomes covered
W1 Young Scientists
  • ✓ Collect evidence by making observations when trying to answer a science question, using first-hand experience.
  • ✓ Make and record observations, and take simple measurements.
  • ✓ Ask questions and suggest ways to answer them, and predict what will happen before deciding what to do.
  • ✓ Recognise that a test or a comparison may be unfair.
  • ✓ Make comparisons, identify simple patterns, and talk about predictions, the outcome, and why things happened.
  • ✓ Talk about risks and how to avoid danger, and communicate ideas using their own scientific experience and understanding.
Living things · 14 outcomes in 3 packs
PackGrade 1 learning outcomes covered
A1 Living Things and What They Need
  • ✓ Name the characteristics of all living things and cite examples of how these are shown in local organisms — how plants and animals grow, reproduce, respire, move and respond to stimuli, excrete, circulate fluids and obtain food.
  • ✓ Identify and describe examples of the external features and basic needs of living things.
  • ✓ Analyse how investigations can help us understand how living things survive.
  • ✓ Identify and describe the changes in living things.
A2 Habitats of Bangladesh
  • ✓ Describe the different types of habitat found in Bangladesh — aerial, arboreal, aquatic, terrestrial and fossorial — and make comparisons between them.
  • ✓ Make observations of a habitat in a local ecosystem and identify the living organisms occupying it.
  • ✓ Explain how the habitat observed supports the characteristics of the living things found there.
  • ✓ Identify similarities and differences between local environments, and know some of the ways these affect the animals and plants found there.
  • ✓ Describe how different places meet the needs of living things.
A3 Healthy Bodies
  • ✓ Define a balanced diet — one with sufficient fibre and the various nutrients, carbohydrates, proteins, fats, vitamins, minerals and water, to maintain good health.
  • ✓ Use the dietary guidelines for Bangladesh to make suggestions for improving the composition of sample meals.
  • ✓ Discuss the importance of regular physical activity to maintaining good health, and give examples of ways to be active every day.
  • ✓ Discuss good hygiene practices and the importance of good hygiene in maintaining a healthy body.
  • ✓ Discuss the importance of rest and recreation for emotional wellness and the maintenance of good health.
Earth and space · 13 outcomes in 3 packs
PackGrade 1 learning outcomes covered
B1 The Sun, Moon and Stars
  • ✓ Explore how the Sun appears to move during the day, and how shadows change.
  • ✓ Model how the spin of the Earth leads to day and night — for example with different sized balls and a torch.
  • ✓ Observe, describe and predict patterns in the motion of the Sun, Moon and stars in the sky.
  • ✓ Identify the appearance of the Sun, Moon and stars — sunrise and sunset, moonrise and moonset.
  • ✓ Discuss how the Sun and the Moon are important in different cultures, with respect to customs and traditions.
B2 Weather Watching
  • ✓ Observe and talk about the weather, recording reports of weather data.
  • ✓ Identify different aspects of patterns and cycles in the environment around them.
  • ✓ Identify water as a resource that cycles through the environment.
B3 Keeping Our Environment Clean
  • ✓ Identify a healthy environment — clean and controlled land use, a controlled system for disposing of human waste, natural forest and vegetation, clean waterways, and clean air rich in oxygen.
  • ✓ Identify different human activities that can degrade the quality of the environment.
  • ✓ Plan and carry out activities that can help improve the quality of the environment.
  • ✓ Suggest ways the school community can help keep the local environment healthy.
  • ✓ Segregate waste according to the material it is made from, and reuse, reduce and recycle materials.
Physical and chemical science · 16 outcomes in 4 packs
PackGrade 1 learning outcomes covered
C1 Materials and Their Shapes
  • ✓ Know that some materials occur naturally and others are made by people.
  • ✓ Recognise different materials, such as plastics and wood.
  • ✓ Know how the shapes of some materials can be changed by squashing, bending, twisting and stretching.
C2 Heating, Cooling and Dissolving
  • ✓ Explore and describe the way some everyday materials change when they are heated or cooled.
  • ✓ Investigate how liquids and solids respond to changes in temperature, for example, water changing to ice, or melting chocolate.
  • ✓ Recognise that some materials can dissolve in water.
  • ✓ Describe matter as everything that has mass and occupies volume.
C3 Light and Sound
  • ✓ Identify different light sources, including the Sun, and know that darkness is the absence of light.
  • ✓ Be able to identify shadows.
  • ✓ Identify how light and sound are produced by a range of sources, and recognize different properties of light and sound through their senses.
  • ✓ Explore how different sounds may be made by making a variety of materials vibrate.
C4 Electricity
  • ✓ Recognise the components of simple circuits involving cells.
  • ✓ Know how a switch can be used to break a circuit.
  • ✓ Recognize how a complete circuit allows for the flow of electricity.
  • ✓ Investigate how different energy sources generate electricity.
  • ✓ Investigate different electrical conductors and insulators.
