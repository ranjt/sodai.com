★★☆
Grade 3
Name:Class:Date:
Learning goals
  • Plan a fair test.
  • Explain repeats.
1[3]
Lina wants to know which cup keeps cha hot longest. She puts hot cha in a clay cup and cold cha in a steel cup. Is it fair? How can she fix it?
No: the starting temperature is different. She must start both with the same amount of cha at the same temperature.
2[2]
Why should you repeat a measurement three times?
To check the results and make them more reliable.
3[2]
Write a prediction with a reason: "Will a plant grow taller in sunlight or in shade?"
I think it will grow taller in sunlight because plants need light to make food.

Testing umbrellas for the monsoon

★★★
Grade 3
Name:Class:Date:
Learning goals
  • Plan a real test.
  • Use evidence to choose.
ScienceDesignMaths

The monsoon rains are coming. A shopkeeper in Rajshahi wants to sell the best umbrellas. He asks a Grade 3 class to test three kinds of cloth: cotton, plastic sheet and nylon.

The class drips 50 drops of water onto each cloth stretched over a cup, then measures how much water gets through.

A story about a class investigation. Results are example data.
1[3]
Complete the plan.
Changethe type of cloth
Measurewater that gets through (ml)
Keep the samenumber of drops, size of cloth, cup
2[2]
Results: cotton 8 ml, plastic 0 ml, nylon 1 ml. Which cloth should the shopkeeper use? Why?
Plastic sheet (or nylon): almost no water gets through.
3[1]
What else should they test before choosing?
Strength, weight, cost, whether it tears in wind.
