Real-world case study · B1 · Solar system

Bangladesh in space: Bangabandhu Satellite-1

★★★
Grade 3
Name:Class:Date:
Learning goals
  • Learn how satellites help people.
ScienceTechnologyHistory

In May 2018, Bangladesh's first satellite, Bangabandhu Satellite-1, was launched into space on a rocket from the United States. It travels far above the Earth.

The satellite sends TV and internet signals to people all over Bangladesh, even on islands and in hills where it is hard to put cables. Weather satellites from other countries also help Bangladesh see cyclones coming.

Based on information about Bangabandhu Satellite-1.
1[1]
What is an artificial satellite?
A machine people send into space to orbit the Earth.
2[2]
How does the satellite help people in Bangladesh?
It sends TV and internet signals to all parts of the country.
3[2]
Design your own satellite. What job would it do?
My satellite
e.g. a weather satellite with cameras and solar panels to warn of cyclones.
