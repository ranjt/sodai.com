The frogs and the rice field

★★★
Grade 3
Name:Class:Date:
Learning goals
  • Use a food chain to explain a real problem.
ScienceFarmingEnvironment

Frogs live in many rice fields in Bangladesh. They eat insects that damage the rice. Some years ago, many people caught frogs to sell, and many farmers sprayed strong poisons (pesticides).

Fewer frogs meant more insects, so farmers sprayed even more poison. Now many farmers protect frogs and use fewer pesticides, so the frogs can help them again.

Based on information about frogs and pest control in rice farming.
1[1]
Write the food chain from the story.
rice → insects → frogs
2[2]
What happened when there were fewer frogs?
There were more insects that damaged the rice.
3[2]
How can frogs help farmers?
They eat insect pests, so farmers need less poison.
