Why do they live there?

★★☆
Grade 3
Name:Class:Date:
Learning goals
  • Explain adaptations.
1[2]
Why couldn't a fish live in a tree?
It breathes with gills in water and has fins, not legs; it would dry out.
2[2]
How is a duck suited to living on a pond?
Webbed feet for swimming and waterproof feathers.
3[3]

My key

Real-world case study · A3 · Habitats

The baya weaver's hanging nest

★★★
Grade 3
Name:Class:Date:
Learning goals
  • Learn how an animal changes its habitat.
ScienceDesignNature

The baya weaver is a small bird found in Bangladesh. In the rainy season, the male weaves an amazing nest from strips of grass and palm leaves. It hangs from the tip of a branch, often over water.

The nest has a long tube for a door at the bottom. Snakes and big birds find it very hard to reach the eggs. The weaver has changed its habitat to make a safe home for its babies.

Based on the behaviour of the baya weaver (babui pakhi).
1[2]
How does the nest keep the eggs safe?
It hangs from a thin branch over water with a long tube entrance, so snakes and big birds cannot reach it.
2[1]
What materials does the weaver use?
strips of grass and palm leaves
3[3]
Design a safe home for an animal you like. Draw and label it.
My animal home
Any reasoned design with labels showing food, water and shelter.
