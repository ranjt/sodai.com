Simple machines make work easier

★☆☆
Grade 3
Name:Class:Date:
Learning goals
  • Explain what a simple machine is.
  • Find levers in everyday life and explain how they make work easier.
  • Explain how ramps, pulleys and gears help us.

Try to lift the lid off a tin of powdered milk with your fingers. Hard! Now push the end of a spoon under the lid and press down. Easy! The spoon is a simple machine.

A simple machine is a tool with few or no moving parts that makes work easier. It may let us use less force, change the direction of a force, or move something further or faster.

Levers

A lever is a bar that turns on a point called the fulcrum (pivot). You push on one part (the effort) to move a load. The further your effort is from the fulcrum, the less force you need.

Bangladesh connection

A dheki is a long wooden lever used in villages to husk rice, and a tubewell handle is a lever that lifts water.

Ramps (inclined planes)

A ramp is a slope. It is easier to push a heavy load up a ramp than to lift it straight up: you move it further, but with less force. Ramps help wheelchair users, people loading trucks, and cars on hill roads that zig-zag up the slope.

Pulleys and gears

A pulley is a wheel with a rope over it. Pulling down on the rope lifts the load up. Pulleys lift buckets from wells, flags up poles and heavy loads on building sites. Gears are wheels with teeth that turn each other. Bicycles, clocks, egg beaters and rice mills use gears.

From dheki to rice mill

★★★
Grade 3
Name:Class:Date:
Learning goals
  • See how machines change daily life.
ScienceHistoryWork

For hundreds of years, village women in Bangladesh husked rice with a dheki. One person stepped on the end of a long wooden beam. The beam turned on a pivot and the other end lifted, then thumped down onto the rice to knock off the husks. It was slow, hard work.

Today most rice is husked in rice mills. A motor turns gears and belts that work the machines, and one mill can husk tonnes of rice in a day. Some families still use a dheki to make special rice flour for winter pitha.

Based on rice husking in Bangladesh.
