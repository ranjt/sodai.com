Ecosystems of Bangladesh

★☆☆
Grade 3
Name:Class:Date:
Learning goals
  • Explain what an ecosystem is and sort its parts into living and non-living.
  • Describe land and water ecosystems found in Bangladesh.
  • Explain how non-living features decide which plants and animals can live there.

An ecosystem is a place where living things (plants, animals and tiny microbes) live together with non-living things (water, soil, rocks, air, sunlight). They all depend on each other.

Non-living things decide who lives there

The non-living features of a place decide which plants and animals can live there: how much water, light and heat there is, the kind of soil, and whether the water is salty or fresh. Only special trees can grow in the salty mud of the Sundarbans. Coral needs warm, clear, salty water. Water lilies need fresh water and sunlight.

Try this at home

Look at a place near your home: a pond, a garden or a field. List the living and non-living things you can see.

Real-world case study · A4 · Ecosystems

Saving the coral of Saint Martin's Island

★★★
Grade 3
Name:Class:Date:
Learning goals
  • Learn about a special ecosystem.
  • Suggest ways to protect it.
ScienceGeographyCitizenship

Saint Martin's Island is the only coral island in Bangladesh. Around it live corals, colourful fish, sea snails and turtles. Corals are tiny animals that build hard homes that grow very slowly.

Every year many tourists visit. Some walk on the coral, break pieces to take home or leave plastic rubbish. Boats and new hotels also harm the reef. The government has made rules to protect the island.
