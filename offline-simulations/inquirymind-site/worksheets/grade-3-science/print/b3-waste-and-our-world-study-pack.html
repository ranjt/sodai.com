Waste and our world

★☆☆
Grade 3
Name:Class:Date:
Learning goals
  • Explain the differences between waste, compost, recyclable and reusable materials.
  • Give examples of solid, liquid and gas waste from plants, animals and people.
  • Explain how waste harms nature and plan ways to reduce it with the three Rs.

Every day, people throw things away: food scraps, plastic bags, paper, bottles. This is waste. If we do not deal with it well, it pollutes our rivers, land and air.

How waste harms nature

  • Plastic in rivers and drains causes floods and harms fish and turtles.
  • Dirty water from factories pollutes rivers like the Buriganga.
  • Smoke from burning rubbish makes the air unhealthy.
  • Rubbish heaps attract rats and flies that spread disease.

The three Rs

  • Reduce: use less. Take a cloth bag to the market.
  • Reuse: use things again. Use a jar as a pencil pot.
  • Recycle: make old things into new things. Give paper, metal and plastic to the bhangari collector.

And compost your food scraps to make rich soil for plants.

Turning rubbish into compost

★★★
Grade 3
Name:Class:Date:
Learning goals
  • See how a community reduces waste.
ScienceCitizenshipMaths

In Dhaka, a lot of household rubbish is food and vegetable waste. In some neighbourhoods, workers collect this waste from homes and take it to a compost plant.

They pile it up in boxes. Worms and microbes break it down. After about two months it becomes dark, crumbly compost, which is sold to farmers and gardeners. Less rubbish goes to the dump, and farmers get good soil.
