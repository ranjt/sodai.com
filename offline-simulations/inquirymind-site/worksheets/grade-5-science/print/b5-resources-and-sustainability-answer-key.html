2[2]
Why is natural gas called non-renewable, even though it was made by nature?
It took millions of years to form from ancient living things; we use it far faster than it can form again.
3[2]
Explain how banning plastic bags helps both the environment and people in cities.
Fewer bags blocking drains means less flooding; less plastic in rivers and soil harms fewer animals; saves oil used to make plastic.
4[2]
Why does saving vultures matter to people too?
Vultures eat dead animals, removing a source of disease and keeping the environment clean.

Saving the hilsa

★★★
Grade 5
Name:Class:Date:
Learning goals
  • Explain how rules help a renewable resource regenerate.
  • Interpret data over time.
  • Consider different points of view.
ScienceEconomicsMathsEthicsCulture

The hilsa (ilish) is the national fish of Bangladesh. Millions of people depend on catching, selling and eating it. Adult hilsa swim from the sea up rivers such as the Meghna and Padma to lay their eggs. The young fish, called jatka, then swim back to the sea to grow.

In the past, so many jatka and breeding adults were caught that the hilsa catch fell. So the government made rules: catching jatka is banned for several months of the year, fishing is banned for 22 days during the main breeding season in October, and some rivers are sanctuaries. During the bans, poor fishing families receive free rice.

Information from the Department of Fisheries, Bangladesh. Catch figures are approximate.

a)Plot the graph and describe the trend.
The catch increased steadily, almost doubling from 3.0 to 5.7 lakh tonnes.
b)Suggest how the fishing bans helped.
More adults could breed and more jatka could grow up, so the population increased.
2[2]
Why are the bans an example of using a renewable resource sustainably?
Fish can regenerate if enough are left to breed; the bans give them time to grow back, so people can keep fishing in future.
