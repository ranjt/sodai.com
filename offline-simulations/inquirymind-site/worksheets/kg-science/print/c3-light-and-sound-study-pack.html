Learning goals
  • Name things that give light.
  • Name ways to make sounds.
For the grown-up

Read aloud. Turn off the lights for a moment, then switch on a torch. Then make sounds with a drum, a bottle and a shaker.

Light helps us see. Things that give light are light sources.

Sun
lamp
torch
candle
fire
stars

Sound helps us hear. We hear sounds with our ears.

Ways to make sounds

hit a drum
blow a flute
shake a shaker
pluck strings
clap

Sounds get quieter when we move far away from them.

Mother lit a candle and Father switched on a torch. Now Babu could see again. Outside, the stars were shining in the sky.
