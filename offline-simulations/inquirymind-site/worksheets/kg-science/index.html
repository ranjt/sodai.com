Study packs and worksheets

Picture-based science activity packs for KG 1 and KG 2 (ages 4 to 6), in black and white. A grown-up reads each page aloud; children look and talk, circle, match, cut and sort, colour picture graphs, trace words and draw. Every pack has its own answer key.

How to use the packs

Print

A4, black and white, double-sided. Every worksheet starts on a new page, so you can print a single sheet or the whole pack.

Learn

Start with the Look and talk page: read it aloud and talk about the pictures. Notes "For the grown-up" explain each activity.

Practise

Stars show the challenge level: ★☆☆ core, ★★☆ deeper, ★★★ stretch. Marks are shown for each question.

Check

Use the answer key to mark or for self-checking. The last page of each pack is a self-assessment: “Not yet, Nearly, Got it!”

Syllabus coverage map

Every KG learning outcome in the school science continuum (IB PYP Phase 1) and the pack that teaches it. Working scientifically skills are also practised in every pack's investigation.

Working scientifically · 6 outcomes in 1 pack
PackKG learning outcomes covered
W1 Little Explorers
  • ✓ Explore and observe using each of the senses in order to find things out.
  • ✓ Notice and talk about what they see, hear, touch, smell and taste.
  • ✓ Ask questions about the things around them, and make a guess about what might happen.
  • ✓ Make predictions about what will happen and compare what happened with what they predicted.
  • ✓ Make simple comparisons between things they have observed, and communicate their ideas in their own words.
  • ✓ Record the stages in their work in simple ways.
Living things · 26 outcomes in 5 packs
PackKG learning outcomes covered
A1 Living and Non-living Things
  • ✓ Identify, sort and classify living things and non-living things.
  • ✓ Know that plants are living things, and that there are living things and things that have never been alive.
  • ✓ Differentiate between living and non-living things, identifying the visible characteristics of each — growth, feeding, movement and reproduction.
  • ✓ Make observations and identify the basic needs of people, plants and animals — food, water, air, protection and care.
  • ✓ Name cells as the building blocks that make up all living things.
  • ✓ Plan and carry out, or take part in, an investigation into how living things survive.
A2 Animals Around Us
  • ✓ Recognize and identify a range of common animals and plants.
  • ✓ Model the differing movements of living things — for example how rabbits hop, how elephants use their trunks, how dolphins swim.
  • ✓ Know that humans and animals produce offspring which grow into adults.
  • ✓ Describe how animals are a life-sustaining resource for us and other living beings.
  • ✓ Explore ways that different animals and plants inhabit local environments.
A3 Plants Around Us
  • ✓ Name the major parts of a plant, looking at real plants and models.
  • ✓ Know that plants need light and water to grow.
  • ✓ Explore how seeds grow into flowering plants.
  • ✓ Describe how plants are a life-sustaining resource for us and other living beings.
  • ✓ Identify what local plant and animal species need in order to grow.
A4 My Body and My Senses
  • ✓ Recognize and name the main external parts of the human body.
  • ✓ Identify the five major sense organs, give the function of each, and suggest why they are important.
  • ✓ Explore how the senses enable humans and animals to be aware of the world around them.
  • ✓ Identify similarities and differences between themselves and other children.
  • ✓ Identify the general physical changes they have undergone since birth, and link them to the idea of growth.
A5 Healthy Food
  • ✓ Know about the need for a healthy diet, including the right types of food and water.
  • ✓ Match different types of local food to the food groups they belong to.
  • ✓ Give examples of nutritious foods and tell how they contribute to growth and energy needs.
  • ✓ Suggest healthy food choices for balanced meals and for snacks.
  • ✓ Discuss the significance of making healthy food choices.
Earth and space · 13 outcomes in 3 packs
PackKG learning outcomes covered
B1 Day, Night and Seasons
  • ✓ Make observations at different times of the day, week and year to relate the amount of daylight to the time of year.
  • ✓ Identify different aspects of patterns and cycles in their authentic environments — for example weather, night and day, and the seasons.
  • ✓ Make connections between cycles and changes in the environment and other cultural perspectives.
B2 Our Environment
  • ✓ Give examples of human-built and natural environments — cities, villages and towns; forest, mangrove, river, mountain and wetlands.
  • ✓ Identify the basic and common components of the human-built and natural environments they have identified.
  • ✓ Talk about the things in our environment that people use, and recognize different resources in our environment and how humans use them.
  • ✓ Identify that the choices we make can have an impact on the land, water, air and other living things.
  • ✓ Consider different ways to take care of our resources, and how we can reduce, reuse and recycle.
B3 Clean and Safe Spaces
  • ✓ Use observation skills to identify the differences between clean and unclean spaces in their school and homes.
  • ✓ Recognise objects or conditions in spaces that make them safe or unsafe, and explain why and what could be done about it.
  • ✓ Identify and participate in activities that keep spaces clean and safe and help improve the quality of their immediate spaces.
  • ✓ Observe and identify the potential and common dangers in human-built and natural environments, and suggest how best to respond.
  • ✓ Use cause-and-effect thinking to judge what to do when they meet a common danger — obey rules and signs, avoid dangerous situations, think before you act.
Physical and chemical science · 13 outcomes in 3 packs
PackKG learning outcomes covered
C1 Materials Around Us
  • ✓ Use the senses to talk about and explore different materials, and recognize and name common materials.
  • ✓ Identify the materials that everyday objects are made from — a pencil, a toy, a cup — and their use in daily life.
  • ✓ Identify the characteristics of different materials, and sort objects into groups based on the properties of their materials.
  • ✓ Gather, compare, sort, classify, order, interpret and describe observable characteristics and properties.
  • ✓ Observe patterns and draw conclusions from what they notice.
C2 Pushes and Pulls
  • ✓ Understand that pushes and pulls are forces, and distinguish between a push and a pull.
  • ✓ Look for ways they use pushes and pulls in daily life — opening a door, playing with cars.
  • ✓ Explore, talk about and describe the movement of familiar things.
  • ✓ Recognise that when things speed up, slow down or change direction there is a cause.
C3 Light and Sound
  • ✓ Identify different sources of light and sound.
  • ✓ Explore different ways to produce sound using familiar objects and actions — striking, blowing, scraping and shaking.
  • ✓ Identify many sources of sound, and know that we hear when sound enters our ear.
  • ✓ Recognise that as sound travels from a source it becomes fainter.
