mangrove leaves → spotted deer → tiger; algae → fish → crocodile (or algae → crab → kingfisher)

tiger (deer and monkeys) or kingfisher (crabs and fish) or crab
c)What would happen to the kingfishers if all the fish died? Explain.
They would have less food; they would eat more crabs, so crab numbers might fall, or some kingfishers might leave or die.
d)What would happen to the crabs if all the kingfishers died?
There might be more crabs because fewer are being eaten.
2[2]
Why do almost all food chains begin with a green plant?
Only plants can make their own food using energy from sunlight; all the energy in the chain starts there.

When the vultures disappeared

★★★
Grade 4
Name:Class:Date:
Learning goals
  • Use a real food web problem.
  • Interpret data about a falling population.
  • Evaluate solutions.
ScienceHealthMathsCitizenship

Vultures are large birds that eat the bodies of dead animals. They are nature's cleaners: a group of vultures can clean a dead cow in less than an hour, stopping germs from spreading.

Since the 1990s, the number of vultures in South Asia has fallen by more than 95 out of every 100. Scientists found the cause: a medicine called diclofenac that was given to sick cattle. When a treated cow died and vultures ate it, the medicine poisoned the vultures.

With fewer vultures, dead animals lay rotting for longer. More stray dogs and rats came to eat them, and diseases can spread more easily. Bangladesh banned diclofenac for animals in 2010 and set up Vulture Safe Zones in 2014, where vultures are fed safe food and the medicines are checked.
