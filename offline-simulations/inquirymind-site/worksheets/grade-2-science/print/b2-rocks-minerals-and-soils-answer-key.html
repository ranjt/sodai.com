The brick kiln

★★★
Grade 2
Name:Class:Date:
Learning goals
  • Learn how soil is used to make bricks.
  • Think about good and bad effects.
ScienceBuildingEnvironment

Near many towns in Bangladesh you can see tall chimneys. These are brick kilns. Workers dig up clay soil, mix it with water, shape it into blocks and bake them in a very hot fire. The soft clay becomes hard bricks for houses, schools and roads.

But brick kilns can cause problems: they use up good farm soil, and the smoke makes the air dirty. New kilns use cleaner fires and some builders use blocks made without burning.

Based on brick making in Bangladesh.
