Pushes and pulls

★☆☆
Grade 2
Name:Class:Date:
Learning goals
  • Explain that pushes and pulls are forces, and that forces can be measured.
  • Show how forces start, stop and change the shape of things.
  • Describe gravity, friction and weight.

Kick a ball, open a door, pull a rickshaw van, squash some clay. Every time, you use a force. A force is a push or a pull.

kick: push
push
pull the oars
pull the string
hit: push

What forces do

  • make things start moving (push a swing);
  • make things stop (catch a ball);
  • make things go faster or slower;
  • change the direction things move (hit a ball with a bat);
  • change the shape of things (squash, stretch, bend or twist clay).

Measuring forces

We can measure how big a force is with a forcemeter. It has a spring inside. The harder you pull, the more the spring stretches. Forces are measured in newtons (N).

A heavy bag needs a bigger force to lift or move than a light bag. The heavier something is, the more force you need to move it.

Gravity

Gravity is a force that pulls everything down towards the Earth. That is why a mango falls from the tree and a ball comes back down. Your weight is how hard gravity pulls on you.

Friction

Friction is a force that happens when two things rub together. It slows things down. A ball rolls far on a smooth floor, but stops quickly on grass or sand. Friction helps us: our shoes grip the ground so we do not slip, and bicycle brakes stop the wheels.

Motion

Motion means moving: changing position over time. A car that goes further in the same time is moving faster.

The rickshaw van

★★★
Grade 2
Name:Class:Date:
Learning goals
  • Find forces in real life.
ScienceWorkMaths

Kamal drives a rickshaw van that carries vegetables to the market. He pushes the pedals with his feet to make it go. When the van is full of potatoes it is very heavy, and he must push much harder.

When he wants to stop, he squeezes the brakes. The brakes rub on the wheels and friction slows the van down. On a wet road the van slides more, so Kamal goes slowly.

A story about forces at work.
