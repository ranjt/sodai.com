2[2]

Quiet near the hospital

★★★
Grade 2
Name:Class:Date:
Learning goals
  • Think about noise and health.
ScienceHealthCitizenship

Near a hospital in Dhaka there is a sign: "Silent zone. Do not use the horn." Sick people need quiet so they can rest and get better.

But many drivers still honk their horns. Children from a nearby school made posters and talked to drivers. They asked them to keep the area quiet. Some drivers stopped honking.

A story about noise near a hospital.
1[2]
Why do people in hospital need quiet?
So they can rest, sleep and get better.
2[2]
What did the children do?
They made posters and asked drivers not to use their horns.
3[2]
Draw a poster to ask drivers to keep quiet.
My poster
e.g. a car with a cross over the horn: "Please, no horn near the hospital!"
