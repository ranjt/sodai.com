Solids, liquids and gases

★☆☆
Grade 2
Name:Class:Date:
Learning goals
  • Sort materials into solids, liquids and gases.
  • Explain that matter has mass and takes up space.
  • Describe melting, freezing and how water turns to steam and back.

Everything around you is made of matter: your desk, the water in your bottle and even the air. Matter has mass (it has weight) and takes up space. Matter can be a solid, a liquid or a gas.

Heating and cooling change things

  • Melting: when a solid is heated it can turn into a liquid. Ice melts into water; chocolate melts in your hand.
  • Freezing: when a liquid is cooled it can turn into a solid. Water in the freezer becomes ice.
  • Melting is the reverse (opposite) of freezing.
  • When water is heated in a kettle it boils and turns into steam (a gas). When steam touches a cold lid, it turns back into water drops.

Stay safe

Steam and boiling water can burn you. Always ask an adult to help with heating.

Bangladesh connection

In winter in the north of Bangladesh it can be very cold, but water does not freeze outside. It only freezes in a fridge freezer, where it is below 0 °C.

The ice cream seller

★★★
Grade 2
Name:Class:Date:
Learning goals
  • Use melting and freezing in real life.
ScienceFoodMaths

On hot afternoons, an ice cream seller pushes his cart near the school. Inside the cart is a box with thick walls and lots of ice. The ice keeps the ice cream cold so it does not melt.

In the morning he buys ice from the ice factory, where water is frozen in big machines. By evening most of the ice has melted into water.
