My amazing body

★☆☆
Grade 2
Name:Class:Date:
Learning goals
  • Name the outside parts of the body and what they do.
  • Name the main organs inside the body and find them on a body outline.
  • Explain how bones and muscles work together to hold us up and help us move.

Your body is like a wonderful machine. Every part has a job, and all the parts work together so you can run, play, eat, think and grow.

Outside parts

Your head holds your eyes, ears, nose and mouth. Your arms and hands help you hold and carry things. Your legs and feet help you stand, walk and run. Joints such as your elbows and knees let you bend.

Inside parts: organs

OrganIts job
braincontrols your body; helps you think and remember
heartpumps blood all around your body
lungstake in air when you breathe
stomachmashes up your food
intestines (small and large)take goodness from food into your blood; carry away waste
livercleans your blood
kidneysclean your blood and make urine (wee)
bladderstores urine

Bones and muscles

Inside your body is a skeleton made of more than 200 bones. It holds you up, gives you your shape and protects soft parts: the skull protects your brain and the ribs protect your heart and lungs. Muscles are joined to bones. When a muscle pulls, the bone moves. Bones and muscles work together so you can move.

Did you know?

Your heart beats about 100 times every minute, all day and all night, even when you are asleep!

Try this at home

Put your hand on your arm and bend your elbow. Can you feel the muscle get fat and hard? It is pulling the bone.

Key words
organ
a body part with a special job, like the heart
skeleton
all the bones in your body
muscle
pulls on bones to make them move
joint
where two bones meet and bend
