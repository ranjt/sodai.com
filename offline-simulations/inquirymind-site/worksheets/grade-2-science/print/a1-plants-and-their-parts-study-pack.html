Plants and their parts

★☆☆
Grade 2
Name:Class:Date:
Learning goals
  • Name the parts of a plant and say what each part does.
  • Explain that plants need water, light and the right temperature to grow.
  • Explain how water travels from the roots up the stem.

Plants are living things. They grow all around us: rice in the fields, mango trees in the garden, and the shapla flower in the pond. Most plants have the same parts.

PartWhat it does
rootshold the plant in the soil and take in water from the soil
stemholds the plant up and carries water from the roots to the leaves
leavesuse sunlight to make food for the plant
flowermakes seeds, so new plants can grow
fruitholds and protects the seeds

How water travels

Water goes in through the roots. Then it travels up the stem, like water up a straw, to the leaves and flowers.

What plants need

water
light
warmth
soil and minerals
air

Plants need water, light, air, the right temperature (not too hot, not too cold) and minerals from the soil. Without water, a plant wilts (droops). Without light, it turns yellow and weak. Plants need healthy roots, stems and leaves to grow well.

healthy plant
no water: it wilts
Bangladesh connection

Bangladesh is warm and wet, so plants grow very well here. Farmers plant rice when the monsoon rains come.

Try this at home

Plant two bean seeds in cups. Water one every day and do not water the other. What happens after a week?

Mina's rooftop garden

★★★
Grade 2
Name:Class:Date:
Learning goals
  • Use plant science in real life.
  • Solve a problem.
ScienceFoodMaths

Mina lives in a flat in Dhaka. There is no garden, so her family grows vegetables in pots on the roof: tomatoes, chillies and spinach (palong shak). Mina waters the pots every evening.

In April the weather is very hot. Some plants start to wilt in the middle of the day. Mina's mother puts up an old cloth to give some shade, and Mina gives them water early in the morning too. Soon the plants are green and strong again.
