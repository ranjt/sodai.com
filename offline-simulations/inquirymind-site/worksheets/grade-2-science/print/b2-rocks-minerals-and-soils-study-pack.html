Rocks are everywhere: in hills, on beaches, under rivers and deep under your feet. Rocks are made of minerals. Over a very long time, rocks break into tiny pieces that become soil.

Types of rock

RockWhat it is likeWe use it for…
granitevery hard, speckledbuildings, kitchen tops, statues
sandstonemade of sand grains stuck togetherwalls and buildings
marblesmooth, can be polishedfloors, statues
slatesplits into thin flat sheetsroofs, writing boards
chalksoft, white, crumblywriting on boards

Minerals

Minerals are the natural materials rocks are made of. Salt is a mineral. Gold, iron and copper come from minerals in rocks. Shiny gems are minerals too.

From rock to soil

Rain, heat, cold and plant roots crack rocks. Rivers roll stones and rub them smooth and small. Over thousands of years rocks become sand and soil.

What is soil?

Soil is a mixture of tiny bits of rock (sand and clay), dead plants and animals (humus), water and air. It lies in layers.

How we use them

bricks from clay
stone for roads
salt
metals and gems
soil to grow food
Bangladesh connection

Bangladesh has very few rocks on the surface, so builders make bricks by baking clay soil in brick kilns. Stones for roads come from rivers in the north, such as near Sylhet.

The brick kiln

★★★
Grade 2
Name:Class:Date:
Learning goals
  • Learn how soil is used to make bricks.
  • Think about good and bad effects.
ScienceBuildingEnvironment

Near many towns in Bangladesh you can see tall chimneys. These are brick kilns. Workers dig up clay soil, mix it with water, shape it into blocks and bake them in a very hot fire. The soft clay becomes hard bricks for houses, schools and roads.

But brick kilns can cause problems: they use up good farm soil, and the smoke makes the air dirty. New kilns use cleaner fires and some builders use blocks made without burning.

Based on brick making in Bangladesh.
