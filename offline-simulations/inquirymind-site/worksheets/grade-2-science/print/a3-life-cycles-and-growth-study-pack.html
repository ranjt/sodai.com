Growing up

★☆☆
Grade 2
Name:Class:Date:
Learning goals
  • Name the stages as animals grow: egg or baby, young, adult.
  • Compare how people and animals grow.
  • Say which places animals need at each stage.

Every animal starts small and grows. A chick hatches from an egg, a calf is born from a cow, and you were once a tiny baby! The stages of growing up make a life cycle.

A chicken grows up

A frog grows up

A frog starts as an egg in a pond. A tadpole hatches. It lives in water and has a tail. It grows legs and becomes a frog that can live on land and in water.

People grow up too

Babies grow into children. Children grow taller and heavier. They learn to walk, talk and read. Adults can have babies of their own. People do not change shape like frogs: a baby looks like a small person.

Measuring growth

We can measure height with a tape measure, weight with scales, and count age in years. Most children grow about 5 to 6 cm taller every year.

The right home at each stage

Animals need different places as they grow. Frog eggs and tadpoles need pond water. Chicks need a warm nest and their mother. Butterfly eggs are laid on leaves so the caterpillars can eat. A habitat is the place where an animal finds food, water and shelter.

Bangladesh connection

Hilsa fish lay their eggs in rivers. The baby fish grow in the river, then swim to the sea.

Baby turtles on the beach

★★★
Grade 2
Name:Class:Date:
Learning goals
  • Read about a real animal.
  • Think about caring for animals.
ScienceGeographyCaring for nature

On the beaches of Saint Martin's Island and Cox's Bazar, mother sea turtles come out of the sea at night. Each one digs a hole in the sand and lays about 100 eggs, then goes back to the sea.

After about two months, tiny baby turtles hatch and race to the sea. Bright lights and rubbish on the beach can make them lose their way. Volunteers protect the nests and help keep the beach dark and clean.

Based on turtle conservation work on the coast of Bangladesh.
