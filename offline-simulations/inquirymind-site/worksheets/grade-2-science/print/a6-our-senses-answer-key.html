Real-world case study · A6 · Senses

Raju uses his other senses

★★★
Grade 2
Name:Class:Date:
Learning goals
  • Learn how people with sight loss use other senses.
ScienceCaring for others

Raju cannot see, but he goes to school like everyone else. He reads books in Braille: little raised dots that he feels with his fingers. He uses a white cane to feel the path in front of him and listens carefully to the sounds around him.

Raju knows his friends by their voices and can tell what is for lunch by the smell. His classmates help by keeping bags off the floor and telling him when something moves.

A story about a learner with sight loss.
