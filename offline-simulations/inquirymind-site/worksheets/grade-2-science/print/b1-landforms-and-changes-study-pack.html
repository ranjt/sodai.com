Mountains, rivers and islands

★☆☆
Grade 2
Name:Class:Date:
Learning goals
  • Name landforms: mountains, hills, valleys, caves, plains and islands.
  • Find landforms in Bangladesh and in other countries.
  • Explain how wind and water change the land, quickly or slowly.

The land is not flat everywhere. There are high mountains, round hills, deep valleys and islands in the sea. These shapes of the land are called landforms.

LandformWhat it is
mountainvery high land with steep sides
hillraised land, lower and rounder than a mountain
valleylow land between hills or mountains, often with a river
cavea big hole in rock, under the ground or in a hill
plainwide, flat land
islandland with water all around it

Landforms in Bangladesh

Most of Bangladesh is a huge, flat plain, made by rivers. In the south-east there are hills in Chattogram, Rangamati and Bandarban. There are many islands, such as Saint Martin's Island and Bhola. Other countries have different landforms: Nepal has the tallest mountains in the world, including Mount Everest.

Wind and water change the land

Rivers carry away soil from their banks and drop it in other places, making new land called chars. Rain washes soil down hills. Waves wear away the coast. Wind blows sand into dunes on beaches.

Fast and slow changes

Fast changesSlow changes
earthquakes shake the groundrivers slowly wear away rock
volcanoes erupt with hot lavawind slowly moves sand
floods and landslides happen in a daymountains take millions of years to grow

Changes can be good: floods bring rich new soil for crops. They can be bad: river banks fall in and people lose their homes.

Bangladesh connection

Keokradong in Bandarban is one of the highest hills in Bangladesh, about 1000 metres high.

A new island in the river

★★★
Grade 2
Name:Class:Date:
Learning goals
  • Learn about chars in Bangladesh.
ScienceGeographyPeople

In the big rivers of Bangladesh, sand and mud slowly pile up to make new islands called chars. After a few years, grass grows on them. Then families come to live there. They grow rice, keep cows and catch fish.

But the river is always moving. Sometimes it washes part of a char away, and families must move their houses. Char people are very good at changing their plans when the river changes.

Based on life on river chars in Bangladesh.
