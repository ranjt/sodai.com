Real-world case study · A5 · Keeping healthy

Shopon's tiffin box

★★★
Grade 2
Name:Class:Date:
Learning goals
  • Use healthy eating in real life.
ScienceHealthMaths

Every day Shopon buys crisps and a fizzy drink from the shop near school for his tiffin. In the afternoon he feels tired and cannot play football well. His teeth also hurt.

His grandmother says, "Let's make your tiffin at home!" Now Shopon takes a roti with egg, a banana and a bottle of water. He feels full of energy, and he saves money too.

A story about healthy tiffin.
1[2]
Why did Shopon feel tired and have sore teeth?
Crisps and fizzy drinks do not give lasting energy, and sugar hurts teeth.
