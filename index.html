Frequently Asked Questions

What is the delivery charge?+
Delivery is ৳40 per order. Orders above ৳600 get free delivery!
How long does delivery take?+
We deliver within 45 minutes for most areas in Dhaka. Hot food orders are prioritised for speed.
Can I return or exchange items?+
Yes! If you're not satisfied with any item, we offer hassle-free returns within 24 hours. Just call us.
How do I track my order?+
Go to the Track page and enter the mobile number you used to place the order. You'll see all your orders and their live status.
Which areas do you deliver to?+
We currently deliver across Dhaka city. We're expanding fast — check back for updates!
