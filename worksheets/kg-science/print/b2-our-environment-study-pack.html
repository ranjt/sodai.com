Our environment is everything around us. Some places are natural. Some places are built by people.

The river clean-up

★★★
KG
Name:Class:Date:
Learning goals
  • Listen to a story.

The children walked to the river near their school. They saw plastic bags and bottles in the water. "The fish cannot live here!" said Rina.

With their teachers, they picked up the rubbish and put it in bags. Now the river is cleaner, and the children put up a sign: "Keep our river clean!"
