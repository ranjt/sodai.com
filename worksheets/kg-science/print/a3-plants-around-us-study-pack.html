Light or dark?

★★☆
KG
Name:Class:Date:
Learning goals
  • Grow beans in light and in the dark.
  • Compare.
For the grown-up

Plant beans in two cups. Put one cup by the window and one in a dark cupboard. Water both. Look after one week.

1
Guess: which bean plant will grow best? Circle it.
by the window
in the dark
2
After one week, draw both plants.
By the window | In the dark
By the window: green and strong. In the dark: pale, thin and weak.
