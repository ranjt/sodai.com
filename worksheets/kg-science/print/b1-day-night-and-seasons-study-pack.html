Seasons in Bangladesh

In summer it is hot. In the rainy season it rains a lot. In winter it is cool and we wear warm clothes.

summer
rainy season
winter

Families celebrate at special times: Pohela Boishakh welcomes the new year, and Eid begins when people see the new Moon.

Mitu watches the Moon

★★★
KG
Name:Class:Date:
Learning goals
  • Listen to a story.
  • Notice patterns in the sky.

Every night Mitu looked at the Moon from her window. Some nights it was a thin smile. Some nights it was round and bright.

One evening the whole family looked for a very thin new Moon. "There it is!" said Mitu. "Tomorrow is Eid!"
