For the grown-up

Read aloud and act it out: point to your eyes, ears, nose, tongue and hands. Ask the children what each one helps us do.

Scientists find out about the world. You can be a scientist too! We use our five senses.

I see
I hear
I smell
I taste
I touch

Scientists ask questions

"What is it? Why? What will happen if…?" Asking questions helps us learn.

Scientists guess, then try

First we guess what will happen. Then we try it and look closely. Did it happen?

Name:Class:Date:
Learning goals

Rafi had a new magnifying glass. In the garden he looked closely at a tiny ant. "It has six legs!" he said.

Rafi asked, "Where is the ant going?" He followed it to a little hole in the ground. Then he told his friends what he found.
