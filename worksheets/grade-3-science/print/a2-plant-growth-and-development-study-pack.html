A tiny seed can grow into a giant mango tree. How does it happen? Let's follow a plant through its life.

Flowering and non-flowering plants

Flowering plants grow flowers that make seeds: mango, rice, lotus, sunflower, grass. Non-flowering plants do not have flowers: ferns and mosses grow from tiny spores, and pine trees make seeds in cones.

The parts of a flowering plant

The life cycle of a flowering plant

  • A seed germinates (starts to grow) when it has water and warmth.
  • It becomes a seedling with its first leaves.
  • The seedling grows into a mature (adult) plant.
  • The plant makes flowers. Insects or wind carry pollen, and seeds form inside fruits.
  • The seeds are spread, and the cycle starts again.

Measuring growth

We can measure a plant's height with a ruler each week, count its leaves, or measure the width of its stem. A bar chart shows how it grows over time.

A rice farmer's year

★★★
Grade 3
Name:Class:Date:
Learning goals
  • Follow a real crop through its life cycle.
ScienceFarmingMaths

Abdul is a rice farmer in Mymensingh. First he soaks rice seeds in water until they germinate. He plants them close together in a small bed of mud. After about a month the seedlings are moved to the big flooded field.

The rice plants grow tall and green. Tiny flowers appear, and the wind carries pollen. The grains (seeds) grow and turn golden. About four months after planting, Abdul harvests the rice. He keeps some seeds to plant next year.

Based on rice farming in Bangladesh.
