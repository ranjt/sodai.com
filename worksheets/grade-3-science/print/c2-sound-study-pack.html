Sound: vibrations, loudness and pitch

★☆☆
Grade 3
Name:Class:Date:
Learning goals
  • Explain that sounds are made by vibrations.
  • Measure loudness in decibels and describe how sound travels.
  • Explain pitch and how musical instruments change it.

Put your fingers on your throat and hum. Can you feel a tingle? That is a vibration: a very fast shaking. Every sound is made by something vibrating.

  • When you hit a dhol, its skin vibrates.
  • When you pluck an ektara, its string vibrates.
  • When you blow a bansuri, the air inside it vibrates.
  • When you talk, your voice box in your throat vibrates.

How sound travels

The vibrations pass from one tiny particle to the next until they reach your ear. Sound can travel through gases (air), liquids (water) and solids (wood, metal, string). It travels better through solids: put your ear on a desk while a friend taps the other end! Sound cannot travel through empty space, where there is no air.

How loud?

Loudness (volume) is measured in decibels (dB) with a sound level meter. Many mobile phones have a sound meter app. Sounds louder than about 85 dB can damage your hearing if you listen for a long time.

Soft, thick materials such as foam, cloth, curtains and egg boxes soak up sound. They stop it from travelling through. This is called sound insulation. Ear defenders use it to protect workers' ears.

High and low: pitch

Pitch is how high or low a sound is. A bird tweets with a high pitch; a big drum booms with a low pitch. High and low sounds can each be loud or soft.

Real-world case study · C2 · Sound

Too much noise!

★★★
Grade 3
Name:Class:Date:
Learning goals
  • Use what you know about sound to solve a real problem.
ScienceHealthCitizenship

Dhaka is one of the noisiest cities in the world. On busy roads the sound is often above 85 dB because of car horns, buses, building work and loudspeakers. Traffic police stand in this noise all day.

Too much noise can damage hearing and make it hard to sleep, learn and think. Bangladesh has rules: very loud hydraulic horns are not allowed, and roads near schools and hospitals are silent zones where drivers must not honk.

Based on reports about noise pollution in Dhaka and Bangladesh's noise rules.
