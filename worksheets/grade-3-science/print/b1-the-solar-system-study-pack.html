On a clear night, look up. You may see the Moon, thousands of stars and maybe a bright planet. The Earth is one of the planets that travel around our star, the Sun. Together they make the solar system.

The eight planets

In order from the Sun: Mercury, Venus, Earth, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, Neptune. A way to remember: "My Very Easy Method Just Speeds Up Naming." Jupiter is the biggest. Saturn has beautiful rings. Earth is the only planet we know with living things.

How it works

The planets travel around (orbit) the Sun. Gravity from the Sun keeps them in their paths. The Earth takes one year to go once around the Sun. The Moon orbits the Earth.

Scientists who studied the sky

  • Aryabhata (India, about 1500 years ago) said that the Earth spins, which makes the stars seem to move.
  • Copernicus (Poland, 1543) said the planets go around the Sun, not around the Earth.
  • Galileo (Italy, 1610) used a telescope and discovered four moons going around Jupiter.
  • Dr Jamal Nazrul Islam (Bangladesh, 1939–2013) was a famous scientist from Chattogram who wrote about how the universe began and how it might end.
Stay safe

Never look at the Sun, even through sunglasses or a telescope. It can damage your eyes.

Bangladesh in space: Bangabandhu Satellite-1

★★★
Grade 3
Name:Class:Date:
Learning goals
  • Learn how satellites help people.
ScienceTechnologyHistory

In May 2018, Bangladesh's first satellite, Bangabandhu Satellite-1, was launched into space on a rocket from the United States. It travels far above the Earth.

The satellite sends TV and internet signals to people all over Bangladesh, even on islands and in hills where it is hard to put cables. Weather satellites from other countries also help Bangladesh see cyclones coming.
