Real-world case study · B3 · Waste

Turning rubbish into compost

★★★
Grade 3
Name:Class:Date:
Learning goals
  • See how a community reduces waste.
ScienceCitizenshipMaths

In Dhaka, a lot of household rubbish is food and vegetable waste. In some neighbourhoods, workers collect this waste from homes and take it to a compost plant.

They pile it up in boxes. Worms and microbes break it down. After about two months it becomes dark, crumbly compost, which is sold to farmers and gardeners. Less rubbish goes to the dump, and farmers get good soil.

Based on community composting projects in Dhaka (example numbers below).
