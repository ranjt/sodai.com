Why do cooking pots have wooden or plastic handles?
Wood and plastic are insulators, so the handle does not get too hot to hold.
2[2]
Why do we wear several layers of clothes in winter?
Air is trapped between the layers; air is an insulator, so it keeps our body heat in.

Keeping warm safely in winter

★★★
Grade 3
Name:Class:Date:
Learning goals
  • Use what you know about heat to stay safe.
ScienceHealthCommunity

In winter, nights in the north of Bangladesh can get very cold, below 10 °C. Many families sit around small open fires to keep warm.

Every winter, some people are badly burnt when loose clothes such as a sari, orna or shawl catch fire. Doctors and fire fighters teach families how to keep warm safely: wear warm layers and use quilts, sit at a safe distance from fires, tie back loose clothes, and never leave a fire alone.

Based on winter safety advice in Bangladesh.
1[1]
How does a fire warm people who are sitting near it?
by radiation: heat rays travel from the fire
2[3]
Write three safety rules for sitting near a fire.
sit at a safe distance; tie back loose clothes; never leave the fire alone; keep water or sand nearby
3[3]
If someone's clothes catch fire, they should stop, drop and roll. Draw the three steps.
Stop, drop, roll
stop moving; drop to the ground; roll over to put out the flames.
