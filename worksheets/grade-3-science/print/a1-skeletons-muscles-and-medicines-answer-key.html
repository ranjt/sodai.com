What happens to the biceps when you bend your arm?
It contracts (gets shorter and fatter) and pulls the lower arm up.

Why do muscles have to work in pairs?
A muscle can only pull, so another muscle is needed to pull the bone back.

Strong bones from small fish

★★★
Grade 3
Name:Class:Date:
Learning goals
  • Link food to strong bones.
  • Use numbers.
ScienceHealthFoodMaths

Bones need calcium to grow strong. Milk, yoghurt (doi) and green leafy vegetables have calcium. In Bangladesh, tiny fish such as mola and dhela are eaten whole, with their bones, so they give lots of calcium too.

Scientists and nutrition workers in Bangladesh have encouraged farmers to grow mola fish in their ponds, so that children get more calcium and vitamins.

Based on nutrition research about small fish in Bangladesh.
