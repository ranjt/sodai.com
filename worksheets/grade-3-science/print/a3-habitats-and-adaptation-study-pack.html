Habitats and adaptation

★☆☆
Grade 3
Name:Class:Date:
Learning goals
  • Explain that animals live in habitats that suit them.
  • Use a simple key to name living things.
  • Explain how living things change their surroundings to meet their needs.

A habitat is the place where an animal or plant lives. It gives the animal food, water, shelter and a safe place to raise young. Different animals live in different habitats.

Suited to their homes

Animals have body parts that help them live in their habitat. This is called adaptation.

AnimalHabitatHow it is suited
fishriver, pond, seagills to breathe in water; fins to swim
monkeytreeslong arms and a tail for climbing
duckpondwebbed feet for swimming; waterproof feathers
earthwormsoilthin, slimy body for sliding through soil
camelhot desertstores fat in its hump; wide feet on sand

Using a key

A key asks yes/no questions to help you name a living thing. Start at the top and follow the answers.

Changing the habitat

Some animals change their surroundings to meet their needs. The baya weaver bird weaves a hanging nest from grass. Termites build tall mounds of mud. Earthworms dig tunnels. People build houses, dig ponds and plant gardens.

birds build nests
ants dig tunnels
worms make burrows
people build houses
Bangladesh connection

In the Sundarbans, fiddler crabs dig holes in the mud to hide when the tide comes in.

Real-world case study · A3 · Habitats

The baya weaver's hanging nest

★★★
Grade 3
Name:Class:Date:
Learning goals
  • Learn how an animal changes its habitat.
ScienceDesignNature

The baya weaver is a small bird found in Bangladesh. In the rainy season, the male weaves an amazing nest from strips of grass and palm leaves. It hangs from the tip of a branch, often over water.

The nest has a long tube for a door at the bottom. Snakes and big birds find it very hard to reach the eggs. The weaver has changed its habitat to make a safe home for its babies.

Based on the behaviour of the baya weaver (babui pakhi).

2[1]
