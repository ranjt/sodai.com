1[2]
How is a plant life cycle like a chicken's? How is it different?
Same: both start small (seed/egg), grow into adults and make new young. Different: plants make seeds in fruits; chickens lay eggs.
2[2]
A seed is kept dry in a packet. Will it germinate? Why?
No: it needs water (and warmth) to germinate.

A rice farmer's year

★★★
Grade 3
Name:Class:Date:
Learning goals
  • Follow a real crop through its life cycle.
ScienceFarmingMaths

Abdul is a rice farmer in Mymensingh. First he soaks rice seeds in water until they germinate. He plants them close together in a small bed of mud. After about a month the seedlings are moved to the big flooded field.

The rice plants grow tall and green. Tiny flowers appear, and the wind carries pollen. The grains (seeds) grow and turn golden. About four months after planting, Abdul harvests the rice. He keeps some seeds to plant next year.

Based on rice farming in Bangladesh.

5harvest the grains

2[1]
Why does Abdul keep some seeds?
To plant next year: the life cycle starts again.
3[1]

about 16–17 weeks
