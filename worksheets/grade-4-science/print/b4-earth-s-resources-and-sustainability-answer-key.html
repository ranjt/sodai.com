Help or hindrance?

★★☆
Grade 4
Name:Class:Date:
Learning goals
  • Evaluate technologies.
  • Explain sustainable choices.
1[3]
Tube-wells gave millions of people in Bangladesh safe water, but some wells have arsenic. Is the tube-well a help or a hindrance? Explain both sides.
Help: it stopped many diseases from dirty pond water. Hindrance: some water has arsenic, which is a poison, and pumping lowers the groundwater. With testing and safe wells it is mostly a help.
2[2]
Why is it a problem if people catch fish faster than the fish can breed?
There will be fewer and fewer fish until they run out, so there is no food or work later.
3[2]
Natural gas will run out. Suggest two other ways Bangladesh could make electricity. Are they renewable?
Solar panels and wind turbines (renewable); also water power (renewable).
4[2]
Give an example of a sudden change and a gradual change to a resource.
Sudden: a cyclone destroys a forest. Gradual: groundwater gets lower each year.

Real-world case study · B4 · Resources

Catching the rain on the coast

★★★
Grade 4
Name:Class:Date:
Learning goals
  • Understand a real water problem.
  • Calculate rainwater from a roof.
  • Evaluate a sustainable solution.
ScienceMathsHealthDesign

In the south-west coast of Bangladesh, in places like Satkhira and Khulna, the water in ponds and under the ground has become salty. Sea water floods in during cyclones and moves into the ground. Drinking salty water is bad for health, so many women and girls walk long distances each day to fetch fresh water.

One solution is rainwater harvesting. Rain falls on a roof, runs into a gutter and pipe, and is stored in a big clean tank. The monsoon brings plenty of rain, so a family can store water to drink during the dry season. Rain is a renewable resource that cycles through the environment.

Based on information about water salinity and rainwater harvesting in coastal Bangladesh.
1[3]

2[2]
Why is rainwater safer to drink than the pond water on the coast?
Rain is fresh water (not salty); stored in a clean, covered tank it stays free of salt and germs.
3[2]
How does a rainwater tank help girls and women?
They do not need to walk far to fetch water, so they have more time for school, work and rest.
4[3]
Design a rainwater harvesting system for your school. Label the roof, gutter, pipe, filter and tank.
Rainwater system
Roof → gutter → pipe → simple filter (cloth/sand) → covered tank with a tap; first flush thrown away.
