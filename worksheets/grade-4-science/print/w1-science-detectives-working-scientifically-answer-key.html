Is it fair?

★★☆
Grade 4
Name:Class:Date:
Learning goals
  • Spot unfair tests.
  • Write predictions.
  • Find patterns.
1[3]
Sami wants to find out which paper towel soaks up the most water. He uses a big piece of towel A and a small piece of towel B, and dips them for different times. Is this fair? Explain how to make it fair.
No: he changed the size and the time as well as the type of towel. Use pieces of the same size, dip them for the same time in the same amount of water, and change only the type of towel.
2[2]
Write a prediction with a reason: "Will an ice cube melt faster in the sun or in the shade?"
I think it will melt faster in the sun because the sun gives it more heat.
3[2]
Look at the results. What is the pattern?
Amount of water each day (ml)02550100
Height of bean plant after 2 weeks (cm)291516
The more water the plant gets, the taller it grows, but above 50 ml it hardly grows any taller.
4[2]
Three children timed the same race: 12 s, 13 s and 25 s. Which result is odd? What should they do?
25 s is odd (maybe the stopwatch was started late); they should time the race again.

Dr Firdausi Qadri: science that saves lives

★★★
Grade 4
Name:Class:Date:
Learning goals
  • Learn how a real scientist uses evidence.
  • Read data from a trial.
  • Think about how science helps people.
ScienceHealthMathsCitizenship

Cholera and typhoid are diseases spread by dirty water. They can make children very ill. Dr Firdausi Qadri, a scientist at icddr,b in Dhaka, has spent her life finding ways to protect people from them.

Scientists cannot just guess that a vaccine works; they need evidence. So Dr Qadri and her team ran large trials with thousands of volunteers in Dhaka. Some people got the vaccine and others did not, and the team counted how many in each group became ill. The evidence helped make cheap vaccines available to millions of children. In 2021 she won the Ramon Magsaysay Award, one of Asia's top prizes, for her work.

Based on information about Dr Firdausi Qadri and icddr,b. Numbers below are example data.
1[3]

a)Draw the bar chart.
b)How many times more people became ill without the vaccine?

2[2]
Why did the trial need two groups?
To compare: the group without the vaccine shows what would happen anyway, so we can see if the vaccine makes a difference.
3[2]
Why was it important to test thousands of people, not just ten?
With many people, the results are more reliable and not just luck.
4[2]
Besides vaccines, how can people protect themselves from diseases spread by dirty water?
Drink boiled or treated water; wash hands with soap; use clean toilets.
