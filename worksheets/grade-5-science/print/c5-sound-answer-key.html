Too loud! Noise in Dhaka

★★★
Grade 5
Name:Class:Date:
Learning goals
  • Use data about loudness.
  • Explain health effects of noise.
  • Evaluate solutions from different viewpoints.
ScienceHealthMathsCitizenshipEthics

In 2022, a United Nations Environment Programme report named Dhaka as one of the noisiest cities in the world. Car and bus horns, construction machines, loudspeakers and generators fill the streets with sound.

Doctors say that living with loud noise every day can cause hearing loss, headaches, stress, high blood pressure and poor sleep. Children in noisy schools find it harder to concentrate and learn.

Bangladesh has noise rules: areas around hospitals, schools and offices are meant to be silent zones, with limits as low as 50 dB in the daytime, and very loud hydraulic horns are banned. But the rules are often broken.

Sources: UNEP Frontiers Report 2022; Bangladesh Noise Pollution (Control) Rules 2006.

2[2]
Explain why a loud horn could harm the ear, using what you know about the eardrum.
Very big vibrations make the eardrum and the tiny parts of the cochlea vibrate very strongly, which can damage them.
