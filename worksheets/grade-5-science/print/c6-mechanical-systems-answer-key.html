The rickshaw: a city on three wheels

★★★
Grade 5
Name:Class:Date:
Learning goals
  • Analyse a real mechanical system.
  • Explain how forces power and stop it.
  • Consider people, jobs and technology change.
ScienceDesignEconomicsEthicsArt

Dhaka has hundreds of thousands of cycle rickshaws, famous for their colourful hand-painted art. A rickshaw is a mechanical system: the puller pushes the pedals (levers), which turn a crank and gear wheel. A chain carries the turning to a small gear on the back axle, turning the big wheels. Brake levers press pads against the wheels, and friction slows them down.

Pulling a rickshaw is hard work, especially with three passengers or on a bridge. In recent years many rickshaws have been fitted with batteries and electric motors. They are faster and easier for the puller, but they can be dangerous at high speed, the batteries must be charged with electricity, and old batteries can pollute if not recycled.

Based on reports about rickshaws and battery rickshaws in Bangladesh.
