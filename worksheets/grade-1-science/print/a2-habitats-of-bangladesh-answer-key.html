Homes in the Sundarbans

★★★
Grade 1
Name:Class:Date:
Learning goals
  • Read about a special habitat.

The Sundarbans is a big forest by the sea. Its trees grow in salty mud. Tigers and deer walk on the land. Monkeys climb the trees.

Crabs dig holes in the mud. Fish and crocodiles swim in the rivers. Birds fly above. It has every kind of habitat!

1
Find an animal for each habitat in the story.

terrestrial (land)tiger or deer
arboreal (trees)monkey
fossorial (underground)crab

aerial (air)birds
