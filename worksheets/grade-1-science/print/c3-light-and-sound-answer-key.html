Shadow puppets

★★★
Grade 1
Name:Class:Date:
Learning goals

At the village fair, a man put on a shadow puppet show. He held cut-out puppets between a lamp and a white cloth.

The audience saw dark shadows of a tiger, a king and a boat on the cloth. A drummer played music. Everyone cheered!

1
What was the light source?
the lamp
2
Why were the shadows on the cloth?
The puppets blocked the lamp's light.
3
How did the drummer make sound?
He hit the drum, so it vibrated.
