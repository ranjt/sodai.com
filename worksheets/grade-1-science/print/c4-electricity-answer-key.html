Real-world case study · C4 · Electricity

Light from the sun

★★★
Grade 1
Name:Class:Date:
Learning goals
  • Read about solar electricity.

Shuvo lives on a char, an island in a big river. There are no electricity wires there.

His family has a solar panel on the roof. In the day it turns sunlight into electricity and stores it in a battery. At night the battery lights two lamps, so Shuvo can do his homework.

1
Where does Shuvo's electricity come from?
  • the Sun
  • a wall socket far away
2
Why is a solar panel useful on the char?
There are no electricity wires, but there is sunshine.
