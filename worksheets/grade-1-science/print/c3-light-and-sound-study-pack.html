Light, dark and sound

★☆☆
Grade 1
Name:Class:Date:
Learning goals
  • Name light sources and explain darkness.
  • Explain shadows and how sounds are made.

We need light to see. Things that give out light are light sources. The biggest is the Sun.

Sun
lamp
torch
candle
fire
screen

Darkness is when there is no light. In a dark room we cannot see anything. The Moon is not a light source: it reflects light from the Sun.

Shadows

When something blocks the light, a dark shadow forms behind it. Shadows have the same shape as the thing blocking the light.

Sound

Sounds are made when things vibrate: shake very fast. A drum skin vibrates when you hit it. A string vibrates when you pluck it. We hear sounds with our ears.

guitar
bell
speaker
barking dog
