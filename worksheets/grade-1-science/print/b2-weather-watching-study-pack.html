Patterns in the weather

In Bangladesh, the weather follows a pattern through the year: hot summer, the wet rainy season (monsoon), and cool winter. Weather forecasters use patterns to tell us what tomorrow will be like.

Key words

The monsoon comes

★★★
Grade 1
Name:Class:Date:
Learning goals
  • Read about the rainy season.

In June, dark clouds fill the sky over Bangladesh. Then the rain comes: the monsoon! Rivers fill up and the fields turn green.

Farmers plant rice. Children float paper boats. But too much rain can bring floods, so people listen to the weather forecast on the radio.

1
Write one good thing about the monsoon.
2
Write one problem the monsoon can bring.
