A day for Tumpa

★★★
Grade 1
Name:Class:Date:
Learning goals
  • Read a story.
  • Give healthy advice.

Tumpa watches cartoons until late at night. In the morning she is sleepy and skips breakfast.

At school she feels tired and cannot play. Her teacher says, "Your body needs sleep, good food and exercise to work well."

1
Give Tumpa two tips to feel better.
Go to bed early (sleep about 10 hours); eat breakfast; play outside.
2
Why does Tumpa feel tired?
  • She did not sleep or eat enough.
  • She ate too much fruit.
