Keeping our environment clean

★☆☆
Grade 1
Name:Class:Date:
Learning goals
  • Describe a healthy environment.
  • Plan ways to keep it clean.

A healthy environment has clean land, clean water, clean air and plenty of trees. People and animals stay well there.

trees and plants
clean rivers
fresh air
clean toilets
rubbish in bins

What harms our environment?

litter
smoke
cutting trees
dirty water in rivers

Sort your rubbish

We can sort waste by what it is made of, so it can be recycled: paper, plastic, metal, glass, and food waste for compost.

paper
plastic
metal
glass
food waste

Remember the three Rs: reduce (use less), reuse (use again) and recycle (make into something new).

The green school

★★★
Grade 1
Name:Class:Date:
Learning goals
  • Read about a school that cares.

At a school in Dhaka, children noticed plastic bags blowing around the playground. They decided to act.

They put labelled bins for paper, plastic and food waste in every class. They planted trees and made a small garden with compost from food waste. Now the school is cleaner and greener!

1
Write two things the children did.
2
Why is food waste good for compost?
