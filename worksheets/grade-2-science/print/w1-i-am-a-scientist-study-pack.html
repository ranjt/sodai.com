I am a scientist!

★☆☆
Grade 2
Name:Class:Date:
Learning goals
  • Use my senses to look closely and ask questions.
  • Make a prediction and test it.
  • Measure, record and tell others what I found.

Scientists are people who are curious about the world. They look closely, ask questions and try things out to find answers. You can be a scientist too!

look closely
ask a question
try it out
measure
record
tell others

1. Look closely

Use your senses: look, listen, touch and smell (but never taste in science!). A hand lens makes small things look bigger.

2. Ask a question

Good questions start with What, Why, How or Which. "Which ball bounces highest?" "Does a plant need water to grow?"

3. Predict

A prediction is what you think will happen. "I think the big ball will bounce highest."

4. Try it out fairly

Change only one thing. Keep everything else the same. If you test balls, drop them from the same height on the same floor.

5. Measure and record

Use simple tools: a ruler for length, a thermometer for how hot or cold, a timer for time and scales for how heavy. Write or draw what you find in a table or a chart.

ruler
thermometer
timer
scales

6. Tell others

Share what you found with drawings, charts and words. Did your prediction come true?

Stay safe

Never taste or smell things in science unless your teacher says it is safe. Wash your hands after touching soil or animals.

Rumi the bird watcher

★★★
Grade 2
Name:Class:Date:
Learning goals
  • Read a true-to-life story.
  • Count and record.
  • Ask questions.
ScienceMathsNature

Rumi lives in a village near a big lake. Every morning before school she sits quietly by the window with her grandfather's old notebook. She looks at the birds in the mango tree and counts them.

Rumi draws each bird and writes the date. She sees that in winter there are many ducks on the lake, but in summer they are gone. "Where do they go?" she asks. Her teacher says, "You are thinking like a scientist!"
