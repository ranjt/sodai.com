Rumi the bird watcher

★★★
Grade 2
Name:Class:Date:
Learning goals
  • Read a true-to-life story.
  • Count and record.
  • Ask questions.
ScienceMathsNature

Rumi lives in a village near a big lake. Every morning before school she sits quietly by the window with her grandfather's old notebook. She looks at the birds in the mango tree and counts them.

Rumi draws each bird and writes the date. She sees that in winter there are many ducks on the lake, but in summer they are gone. "Where do they go?" she asks. Her teacher says, "You are thinking like a scientist!"
