Learning goals

Tanvir visits his grandmother in the village. Behind her house there is a pond. Ducks swim on the water and lotus flowers float on top. Small fish dart under the water. A frog jumps from a lily pad. On the bank there are stones, an old boat and a tree.

Tanvir sits very still and watches. He sees a kingfisher dive and catch a fish!
