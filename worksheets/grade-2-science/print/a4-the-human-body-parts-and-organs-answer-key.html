A visit to the doctor

Learning goals
  • Link body parts to a real visit.

ScienceHealth

Nadia goes to the clinic for a check-up. The doctor uses a stethoscope to listen to her heart and lungs. She measures Nadia's height and weight, and looks in her ears and mouth.

The doctor says, "Your heart is strong and your lungs are clear. You have grown 5 centimetres this year! Keep eating healthy food and playing outside."
