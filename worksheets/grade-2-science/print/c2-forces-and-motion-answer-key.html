The rickshaw van

★★★
Grade 2
Name:Class:Date:
Learning goals
  • Find forces in real life.
ScienceWorkMaths

Kamal drives a rickshaw van that carries vegetables to the market. He pushes the pedals with his feet to make it go. When the van is full of potatoes it is very heavy, and he must push much harder.

When he wants to stop, he squeezes the brakes. The brakes rub on the wheels and friction slows the van down. On a wet road the van slides more, so Kamal goes slowly.

A story about forces at work.
1[1]
What force does Kamal use to make the van go?
a push on the pedals
2[2]
Why is it harder to pedal when the van is full?
The van is heavier, so it needs a bigger force to move.
3[2]
How do the brakes stop the van?
They rub on the wheels; friction slows them down.
