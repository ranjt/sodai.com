Keeping healthy

★☆☆
Grade 2
Name:Class:Date:
Learning goals
  • Name the life processes of humans and animals.
  • Explain how breathing, the heart, digestion and getting rid of waste work together.
  • Describe a healthy diet and why exercise and sleep matter.

People and animals all do the same important things to stay alive. We call them life processes. Taking care of our bodies helps us do them well.

Life processWhat it means
nutritioneating food and drinking water
growthgetting bigger
movementmoving our bodies
reproductionhaving young (babies)

Our body works as a team

  • Digestion: the stomach and intestines break food down into goodness the body can use.
  • Breathing: the lungs take in air with oxygen.
  • Circulation: the heart pumps blood, which carries food and oxygen all around the body.
  • Getting rid of waste: kidneys make urine; the large intestine gets rid of food we cannot use.

If one part does not work well, the others find it harder too. That is why we must look after our whole body.

Eat a healthy, varied diet

Eat lots of vegetables and fruit, some rice, bread or roti for energy, and fish, eggs, dal or meat to help you grow. Drink plenty of clean water.

vegetables
fruit
rice
fish
eggs
water

Some foods can damage our health if we eat too much of them: very sweet foods (sweets, fizzy drinks, cakes) and very fatty foods (fried snacks, chips). They are treats, not everyday food.

sweets
cake
chips
fast food

Move, rest and stay clean

  • Exercise every day: run, play, swim, cycle. It makes your heart, lungs and muscles strong.
  • Sleep about 10 hours a night so your body can rest and grow.
  • Wash your hands with soap before eating and after the toilet. Brush your teeth twice a day.

sleep

Remember

Your body is precious. Look after it: eat well, drink water, play, sleep and keep clean!

Shopon's tiffin box

★★★
Grade 2
Name:Class:Date:
Learning goals
  • Use healthy eating in real life.
ScienceHealthMaths

Every day Shopon buys crisps and a fizzy drink from the shop near school for his tiffin. In the afternoon he feels tired and cannot play football well. His teeth also hurt.

His grandmother says, "Let's make your tiffin at home!" Now Shopon takes a roti with egg, a banana and a bottle of water. He feels full of energy, and he saves money too.
