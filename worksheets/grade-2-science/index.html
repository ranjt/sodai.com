Study packs and worksheets

Print-ready, black-and-white study packs that cover the whole Grade 2 science syllabus. Each pack teaches the topic with an illustrated article, then builds from facts to thinking, a real investigation and a transdisciplinary case study set in Bangladesh. Every pack has its own answer key.

How to use the packs

Print

A4, black and white, double-sided. Every worksheet starts on a new page, so you can print a single sheet or the whole pack.

Learn

Start with the Learn it article and its key words. Learners can read it in class or at home before the activities.

Practise

Stars show the challenge level: ★☆☆ core, ★★☆ deeper, ★★★ stretch. Marks are shown for each question.

Check

Use the answer key to mark or for self-checking. The last page of each pack is a self-assessment: “Not yet, Nearly, Got it!”

Syllabus coverage map

Every Grade 2 learning outcome in the school science continuum (IB PYP Phase 2) and the pack that teaches it. Working scientifically skills are also practised in every pack's investigation.

Working scientifically · 10 outcomes in 1 pack
PackGrade 2 learning outcomes covered
W1 I Am a Scientist
  • ✓ Observe carefully in order to gather data, using the senses to examine objects and living things.
  • ✓ Observe and compare objects, living things and events.
  • ✓ Measure using simple equipment, and record observations in a variety of ways.
  • ✓ Select different tools and explore ways of using them for an investigation.
  • ✓ Identify a question or problem to be explored.
  • ✓ Suggest ideas, make predictions, and communicate them.
  • ✓ With help, think about collecting evidence and planning a fair test.
  • ✓ Interpret the data gathered in order to draw conclusions, sorting and classifying by observable features.
  • ✓ Make generalisations and begin to identify simple patterns in results.
  • ✓ Use scientific vocabulary to explain observations and experiences, and present findings using drawings, charts and tables.
Living things · 25 outcomes in 6 packs
PackGrade 2 learning outcomes covered
A1 Plants and Their Parts
  • ✓ Know that plants have roots, leaves, stems and flowers.
  • ✓ Explain observations that plants need water and light to grow.
  • ✓ Know that water is taken in through the roots and transported through the stem.
  • ✓ Know that plants need healthy roots, leaves and stems to grow well.
  • ✓ Know that plant growth is affected by temperature.
  • ✓ Identify and describe examples of the external features and basic needs of living things.
  • ✓ Analyse how investigations can help us understand how living things survive.
A2 Living and Non-Living Things
  • ✓ Describe the differences between living and non-living things, using knowledge of life processes.
  • ✓ Sort living things into groups using simple features, and describe the reasons for the groupings.
A3 Life Cycles and Growth
  • ✓ Observe the growth cycle of specific animals in order to identify the growth stages.
  • ✓ Describe the typical physical growth cycle of an animal — egg, immature form, mature organism — using labelled line drawings, and measure the height, weight and age of people and other animals.
  • ✓ Identify the habitats needed to support different stages in the growth cycle of a variety of local animals.
  • ✓ Compare the life cycle of human beings with that of a variety of local animal species.
  • ✓ Describe how different places meet the needs of living things.
  • ✓ Identify and describe the changes in living things as they grow.
A4 The Human Body: Parts and Organs
  • ✓ Identify the major external parts of the human body, describe the function of each, and explain how they relate to each other.
  • ✓ Name the major organs of the human body — brain, heart, lungs, stomach, liver, urinary bladder, small and large intestines, kidneys — and identify them on an outline of the human body.
  • ✓ Tell that humans have an internal skeleton and muscles, and demonstrate how the skeleton and muscles work together to support the body and help it move.
A5 Life Processes and Keeping Healthy
  • ✓ Know the life processes common to humans and animals: nutrition, growth, movement and reproduction.
  • ✓ Investigate how basic life processes such as circulation, simple respiration, excretion and digestion interact with each other in order to maintain a healthy body.
  • ✓ Explore and research the exercise and the adequate, varied diet needed to keep healthy.
  • ✓ Know that some foods can be damaging to health, for example very sweet and fatty foods.
  • ✓ Explain how and why they need to appreciate and care for their own body.
A6 Our Senses
  • ✓ Explore the human senses and the ways we use them to learn about our world.
  • ✓ Recognize different properties of light and sound through the senses.
Earth and space · 11 outcomes in 2 packs
PackGrade 2 learning outcomes covered
B1 Landforms and Changes
  • ✓ Identify a variety of the Earth's surface features that are landforms — mountains, valleys, hills, caves, flatlands and islands.
  • ✓ Identify and describe natural landforms found in their local area, within Bangladesh and in other countries, and note the similarities and differences between them.
  • ✓ Discuss how landform changes are brought about, and how those changes can be positive or negative for humans, animals and plants.
  • ✓ Explain how wind and water change the Earth.
  • ✓ Identify events that occur quickly or slowly on Earth — for example volcanic activity, earthquakes and erosion.
  • ✓ Discuss how the environment affects humans and other living things.
B2 Rocks, Minerals and Soils
  • ✓ Recognise some types of rock and the uses of different rocks.
  • ✓ Demonstrate one or more ways by which rocks break down to form the basis of soil.
  • ✓ Describe soils as a combination of different minerals, rocks and organic materials layered together in various ways.
  • ✓ Give examples of how minerals, rocks and soils are used by humans.
  • ✓ Understand that the Earth's resources are used in a variety of ways.
Physical and chemical science · 18 outcomes in 3 packs
PackGrade 2 learning outcomes covered
C1 States of Matter
  • ✓ Know that matter can be solid, liquid or gas.
  • ✓ Describe matter as everything that has mass and occupies volume.
  • ✓ Investigate how materials change when they are heated and cooled.
  • ✓ Know that melting is when a solid turns into a liquid, and that it is the reverse of freezing.
  • ✓ Observe how water turns into steam when it is heated, but on cooling the steam turns back into water.
  • ✓ Investigate how liquids and solids respond to changes in temperature — for example water changing to ice, or melting chocolate.
C2 Forces and Motion
  • ✓ Know that pushes and pulls are examples of forces, and that forces can be measured.
  • ✓ Demonstrate a basic understanding of the concepts of gravity, friction and weight.
  • ✓ Relate the effect of gravity on the movement of an object.
  • ✓ Explore how forces can make objects start or stop moving.
  • ✓ Explore how forces can change the shape of objects.
  • ✓ Explain how friction affects the movement of an object, and explore how forces including friction can make objects move faster or more slowly.
  • ✓ Correlate the weight of an object to the amount of force required to move it.
  • ✓ Understand that motion is a change in position over time.
C3 Hearing and Sound
  • ✓ Name the parts of the ear and explain how the ear detects sounds, using the terms outer, middle and inner ear, eardrum, cochlea, nerve and brain.
  • ✓ Explain why and how hearing is important to humans and animals.
  • ✓ Discuss sources of sound pollution and investigate different ways of insulating against sound.
  • ✓ Identify how light and sound are produced by a range of sources.
